# Short description by repo Author

You live in a world filled with entities called Aniimos and normal Humans. 
Your main goal is to gather as much of them as you can to both fill your collection and to make a strong team for yourself.
World is filled with wild Aniimos which you can either catch or fight against. Beside that game offers boss fights and battles with other players or NPC spread around the world, less popular but still in the game are things like expeditions that have custom rules.
Game does offer multiplayer but mainly just as a visuals to show players to other players, but some activites can bo done with friends.
Sadly without progressing in story mode players can get far since game blocks them from earning levels after reaching lvl cap which can be risen by progressing in story.
Catching and fighting is not the only thing you can do as the game offers chatting/chilling with other players, making/upgrading/customizing your own garden, a lot of events and many more.

# Comparison

Most similar games that I can think of are Palworld and Pokemon (any game). Mainly Palworld but in my opinion this game have much more to offer (and is less brutal).
In all those three games you catch somekind of animals/entities which are being called Aniimos/Pals/Pokemons.
