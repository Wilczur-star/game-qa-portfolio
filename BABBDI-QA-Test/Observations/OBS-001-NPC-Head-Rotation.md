# OBS-001 — NPC head rotates to a visually unnatural angle

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Classification | Observation — further verification required |
| Severity | Low / Observation |

## Environment

- Windows 11
- PC
- Keyboard and mouse

## Observed Behavior

An NPC's head can rotate to a visually unnatural angle of approximately 180 degrees.

## Reason for Classification

The behavior appears unusual, but the game's visual style may make it intentional. It is therefore recorded as an observation rather than a confirmed defect.

## Suggested Follow-Up

- Compare the behavior with other NPCs.
- Repeat it from different player positions.
- Confirm the intended head-tracking limits with design documentation or the development team.
